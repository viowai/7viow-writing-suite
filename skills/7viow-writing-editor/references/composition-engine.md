# New Prose Composition

Use for new continuous prose. Existing-text diagnosis and ordinary revision belong to detect and rewrite. A user's request to rebuild an article can legitimately enter this workflow.

## Choose the writing contract

- Factual article, report, biography, or product explanation: use supplied or verified evidence; separate observations, interpretations, plans, and claims.
- Authorized fiction or fictional examples: invent within the brief, preserve established continuity, and identify fiction when the context could mislead.
- Real-world assertions inside fiction: verify them when they are presented as facts, or make the fictional treatment explicit.

Match the genre's task: a technical guide needs reproducible order; a research report needs evidence and method limits; marketing needs an offer and supported proof; a story needs scene, viewpoint, and continuity. Genre does not authorize fabricated testimonials or personal history.

## 1. Research and core

Define the reader, their existing understanding, the intended effect, and the material available. Research only when the claim needs missing or current evidence. Keep a compact source map for a substantial factual piece.

State the central idea and challenge it with the strongest relevant objection or counterexample. Distinguish what the evidence actually establishes from what the author wants it to establish.

Complete this stage according to the result:

- Holds: the necessary evidence supports the bounded idea and the important objection has an answer.
- Changes: revise the idea and recheck the evidence and objection affected by that revision.
- Breaks: replace the idea or write the supported negative conclusion; do not structure a persuasive article around the rejected claim.

For fiction, test the premise against the brief, viewpoint, and established world rather than demanding external evidence for invented events. A document labeled “research complete” is not itself a completion test.

## 2. Structure

Choose an order that advances the reader's understanding: mechanism and proof, investigation and conclusion, chronology and turning point, or another suitable movement. Give each section a distinct job and keep evidence near the claim it supports.

Allocate length by importance and difficulty, not equal section sizes or a fixed percentage template. Preserve supporting examples and qualifications when compressing. A long piece benefits from an outline; a short piece can keep the outline internal.

If direction is unresolved and user judgment changes the result, show a small outline or opening. If the user asks to draft directly, proceed with a reasonable stated interpretation instead of adding a confirmation gate.

Structure is ready when required material has a place, dependencies are coherent, and the intended reader can follow the progression. An objection that changes the thesis returns to the previous stage.

## 3. Draft

Write the requested unit or complete piece from the accepted or permitted direction. Use concrete evidence, examples, and language that belongs to this subject. Keep factual additions supported; make authorized fictional invention coherent with the premise.

A brief may mix operational facts, directions to the writer and the document's review state. Build recipient copy from the facts that matter to that reader's understanding, choice or next action. A drafting caution about an estimate, for example, can become the supplied scope and price-confirmation condition in client copy. Keep editing instructions and draft-review status in the delivery note; an unconfirmed activity date belongs in the copy because it affects participation. Apply this distinction to each recipient's version, including one intended for the author to read or use.

Use a primary comparison, example, scene, or causal chain when it genuinely carries understanding. Do not manufacture an analogy or a personal mistake to fill a framework.

## 4. Review

Check structure, logic, facts or fictional continuity, and viewpoint. Identify material repetitions by meaning; retain callbacks, recap, new evidence, and necessary qualification when they perform a distinct job.

Resolve defects that invalidate the piece before polishing. For unsupported facts, verify, qualify, remove within scope, or report the gap. A structural defect needs a structural repair rather than more attractive sentences.

## 5. Polish

Read for natural expression, density, rhythm, register, and useful specificity. Use the relevant pattern family when needed. Compare changed passages with their factual source or established continuity; style improvements must not alter their meaning.

## 6. Deliver

Finish when the reader can follow the intended movement, the important claims or fictional events satisfy their contract, and the requested scope is complete. Return the clean candidate with only material unresolved issues. This is editorial readiness, not a claim of user approval or publication.

## Optional controls

For a supplied style sample, note observable sentence behavior, vocabulary, rhythm, viewpoint, and what to preserve. It supplies style, not facts. For extended creative work, keep brief direction, style, continuity, and unresolved-issue notes only as needed.

For platform adaptation, preserve the core, evidence, and reader action while changing length and presentation. SEO, visuals, and durable project memory are separate optional work; use them only when relevant and authorized. Store writing memory only on the user's explicit request.

Honor a requested phase jump when preceding inputs remain valid. Reopen only the assumptions the change invalidates. Show progress when useful for a long task, not a compulsory six-message ritual.

## Existing author decisions and factual cases

Use a supplied or previously accepted voice and information order. Partner may supply an anchor or resolve an open direction; direct composition from a clear brief can proceed without invoking Partner. If exploring the author's preferences becomes the requested main work, transfer that choice with the existing material rather than repeating intake.

For a factual case, distinguish the author's responsibility, their decisions and actions, the team's contribution, the observed result and any sourced reflection or later application. A missing result remains a gap; do not turn release into adoption or invent a success metric. Use the requested narrative form and preserve these distinctions in the finished prose.
