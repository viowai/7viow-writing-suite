# Layered Writing Method Palette

Use this reference when the user asks how a whole piece, chapter, section, paragraph, or sentence should create movement, strengthen an argument, hold attention, or lead to an action. It is especially relevant when the user names a method such as `欲扬先抑`, asks for several writing directions before prose, or says that structurally correct copy still feels flat.

Do not use this palette as a substitute for a writing anchor, a case-narrative structure, or factual evidence.

## 1. Method Scope

Use this practical palette to choose how a piece of writing develops. Its groupings are working choices, not a universal academic standard.

- Several names are familiar Chinese rhetorical or composition terms; use their definitions and boundaries below.
- A method describes how confirmed material moves. It does not prove a claim or authorize stronger wording.
- A method can be correctly selected while the sentences still fail the approved voice. The writing anchor remains authoritative for style.
- Keep method labels in author-facing planning unless the user explicitly requests a methodology document.

## 2. Keep Four Controls Separate

| Control | Governing question | Must not become |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing anchor | How should this unit sound, feel, and relate to the reader? | A rhetorical formula |
| Narrative method | In what causal order should a case or body of evidence unfold? | A sentence-level style instruction |
| Writing method | How should this specific layer create contrast, progression, proof, emotion, or action? | A source of facts or voice |
| Fact boundary | Which claims are supported, restricted, missing, or forbidden? | An obstacle to be written around |

Establish the available facts, accepted or provisional voice, and affected content layer before choosing a method. A missing voice sample does not block a requested structural outline. Preserve an accepted information order unless the user changes that scope.

## 3. Identify the Layer First

| Layer | Typical decision | Useful method scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole artifact | The large loop from opening promise or problem to final return | progression, opening-closing echo, nested loops |
| Chapter | The chapter's reader movement and relation to adjacent chapters | suppress/elevate, contrast and establishment, transition |
| Section | One problem, judgment, or emotional turn | scene resonance, question-led entry, counterproof, stepwise movement |
| Paragraph | The function and order of several sentences | close-up, narrative plus commentary, reference comparison |
| Sentence or sentence group | Rhythm, emphasis, contrast, and landing | strong-line encapsulation, concrete data, short landing line, white space |
| Compact field | One message under a hard length or format constraint | usually one restrained method only |

Do not descend mechanically. If the chapter direction is unsettled, do not polish sentence rhythm as if the chapter were approved.

For long-form work, distinguish the whole-artifact loop from smaller chapter loops. A chapter may create and resolve its own tension while still advancing the larger opening-to-closing movement. A local loop must not reset the article, repeat the same conclusion, or compete with the whole-piece promise.

## 4. Core Palette

### 4.1 Shape: Organize the Movement

| Method | Reader movement | Use when | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `承上启下节点` / bridge and transition | closes the prior unit and creates a reason to continue | two approved units need a meaningful hinge | do not add a new claim merely to connect sections |
| `欲扬先抑（先抑后扬）` / `欲抑先扬（先扬后抑）` | credible pressure makes later value legible, or desire is established before reality interrupts it | the contrast is real and its order matches the reader's needed movement | do not invent failure, shame the subject, or bait the reader with an unsupported promise |
| `层层递进` / progressive escalation | each step increases consequence, specificity, or understanding | several pressures or decisions form a causal build | a longer list is not progression; every step must change the stakes |
| `正反破立` / contrast, challenge, and establish | rejects a tempting but weak framing, then establishes the supported judgment | the reader may hold the wrong default model | do not create a straw opponent or repeat `not X, but Y` as a verbal tic |
| `首尾呼应（章节闭环、首尾对仗）` / opening-closing echo | the ending returns to and advances the opening; a chapter may resolve its question or use natural parallel phrasing | a chapter or full piece needs closure | do not merely repeat the opening, summarize every paragraph, or force symmetry that distorts meaning |

### 4.2 Strengthen: Build the Argument

| Method | Reader movement | Use when | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `参照物法` / reference comparison | understands an unfamiliar point through a relevant comparison | the reference is known and genuinely comparable | state the comparison scope; analogy is not evidence |
| `特写放大` / close-up magnification | sees one concrete moment, object, or pressure in detail | abstract prose needs a human-scale proof point | the detail must come from source material |
| `夹叙夹议` / narrative plus commentary | moves between what happened and what it means | a scene needs author judgment without becoming a lecture | keep event and interpretation distinguishable |
| `设问引路` / question-led movement | follows a real question into the next judgment | the question mirrors reader uncertainty | avoid fake questions whose answers are obvious slogans |
| `反证法` / counterproof | sees why the alternative fails before accepting the proposed judgment | a supported counterexample clarifies necessity | absence of evidence is not proof of failure |

### 4.3 Engage: Hold Emotion and Attention

| Method | Reader movement | Use when | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `制造认知失调` / controlled cognitive dissonance | notices a defensible mismatch between expectation and reality | the contradiction is source-backed and useful | do not manipulate fear or manufacture surprise |
| `视听化叙事` / audiovisual narration | can picture, hear, or sequence the moment | sensory or interface evidence is available | do not imply media or observation that does not exist |
| `场景共鸣` / scene resonance | recognizes a concrete role, pressure, desire, or decision | the audience and situation are known | avoid generic empathy claims or psychological diagnosis |
| `金句封装` / strong-line encapsulation | carries one earned judgment forward | prior material has already established the claim | a strong line cannot outrun evidence or replace explanation |
| `留白与悬念` / white space and suspense | pauses with a meaningful unanswered question or restrained ending | later content will genuinely resolve or extend it | do not withhold required facts or create clickbait |

### 4.4 Ground: Make the Point Actionable

| Method | Reader movement | Use when | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `数据具象化` / data concretization | understands scale, time, cost, or change through verified quantities | the number and its scope are confirmed | never invent, round, or decontextualize a metric for vividness |
| `丑话说在前` / lead with the caveat | understands a real cost, limitation, or condition before commitment | trust depends on an explicit boundary | do not turn public copy into defensive process language |
| `行动呼唤` / call to action | knows the next appropriate action | the surface has a real action and the user authorizes it | do not add sales pressure or imply permission to send, buy, publish, or submit |

## 5. Application Patterns

These are combinations, not additional universal methods.

### Desire -> Pressure -> Responsibility

1. Establish what the person wants and why it matters.
2. Let real constraints interrupt that desire.
3. State what the product, author, or argument must take responsibility for.
4. Land one short, earned judgment.

This may combine scene resonance, elevate-before-suppressing, progressive escalation, and contrast-and-establish.

### Pressure -> Judgment -> Evidence -> Next Meaning

Use for a section that must do more than list features:

1. one concrete friction,
2. one author or product judgment,
3. one nearby piece of evidence,
4. one consequence or bridge to the next unit.

### Admit Boundary -> Convert to Strategy -> Return to the User

Use when a limitation itself explains a design decision. Acknowledge the supported boundary, show the deliberate response, land the judgment, and return to what the user can understand or control.

### Long Sentence Narrates; Short Sentence Lands

Let longer sentences carry scene, causality, or accumulation. Use a short sentence only when the preceding material has earned it. Repeated fragments without buildup create artificial intensity.

### Whole Loop -> Chapter Loop -> Local Landing

1. Name the whole piece's opening pressure, promise, or question.
2. Give each chapter one movement that changes the reader's understanding.
3. Let sections and paragraphs resolve only their local question.
4. Use the closing to return to the opening with a changed meaning, not a repeated summary.

Use this pattern to prevent a long piece from becoming several isolated mini-articles or several copies of the same contrast.

## 6. Preserve Human Intent Before Applying a Method

Before changing a user-authored draft, map each meaningful element to one treatment:

| Treatment | Meaning | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `preserve` | Keep wording or meaning in place | Record whether the lock is exact or semantic |
| `reorganize` | Move confirmed material to improve sequence | Do not rewrite voice or claims unless separately authorized |
| `weaken` | Reduce an unsupported or overly strong claim | Keep the intended point visible when evidence allows |
| `internalize` | Keep the idea in author-side reasoning but remove the label or explanation from public copy | Do not silently delete the underlying judgment |

A rhetorical method operates on this mapped material. It does not authorize deleting a human concern because it does not fit the chosen shape.

### Selective emphasis

When the target surface supports emphasis, bold or visually stress only words that carry a real judgment, contrast, or decision. Emphasis is a presentation decision, not an additional writing method. Do not bold every summary, decorate generic nouns, or use typography to manufacture importance that the prose has not earned.

## 7. Direction-Generation Protocol

1. Identify the current layer, reader, intent, available facts, and any accepted anchor or preserved dimensions. An absent voice anchor does not prevent a provisional structural outline.
2. Name the reader movement needed in plain language before naming a method.
3. Select the method or combination that creates that movement without competing directions.
4. When the user explicitly asks for a method map or writing directions, propose several genuinely different movements and mark one recommendation with a reason.
5. Match the options to the real choices and the user’s request; different labels for the same movement are not alternatives.
6. Make each option concrete with a micro-example or outline and the tradeoff that matters. Explain preserved material or risks when they affect the choice.
7. For a decision-only request, present the concrete choice and recommendation. If the user permits you to choose and continue, proceed without an additional method-approval step.
8. Once a direction is chosen or permitted provisionally, draft within the user's requested scope.
9. Develop layers in dependency order. Distinguish permission to continue drafting from final approval, without adding confirmations for already authorized work.

For ordinary voice negotiation, offer only enough distinct samples to resolve the current uncertainty.

## 8. Anchor Precedence and Style Drift

A correct method map does not guarantee acceptable prose. If structure and method are approved but the sentences feel flat, mechanical, too cold, or unlike the approved opening:

1. Keep the approved layer, order, and method locked.
2. Reopen only wording, tone, rhythm, or reader relationship.
3. Re-read the nearest approved human sample.
4. Extract observable traits: emotional heat, action verbs, conflict, sentence length, punctuation, questions, and landing lines.
5. Rewrite one sentence group and compare it with the anchor before continuing.

Do not solve style drift by changing the chapter direction or adding more methods.

## 9. Finish at the affected layer

For voice drift, compare the result with the actual sample at paragraph scale while preserving the approved direction. Use [prose-quality-gate.md](prose-quality-gate.md) for stock phrasing, frequency settings and the complete repair check; the method palette here does not exempt a candidate from that gate.

When revising a reply whose exchange is available, begin with what the recipient needs next rather than retelling their own background. A standalone piece still needs the context its audience lacks. This selected style pass supports the agreed writing anchor and preserves this method palette.

The revision creates the intended movement, preserves required human concerns and factual limits, and remains compatible with the accepted anchor. A whole-piece promise and its chapter movements advance one another; a local repair does not reset the article. Judge the actual outline or sentences, not whether a method name or record is present.

When comparing methods is itself the request, show the concrete alternatives. Otherwise deliver the resulting writing. A method map or detailed record is useful only when the user asks for it or another writer needs those decisions.
