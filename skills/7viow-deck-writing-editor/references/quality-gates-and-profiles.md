# Content Review and Presentation Profiles

Use the checks needed to judge the requested route. These are editorial reviews unless a named tool actually ran; do not describe an agent's self-review as deterministic validation.

## Route completion

- Audit: material findings meet “Findings and repair scope” below; valid content is identified and repairs stay within the requested scope.
- Architecture: the audience move is clear; sections and pages have distinct jobs; required sources are allocated; dependencies and key questions are coherent.
- Composition: the requested pages are complete at the declared candidate status, claims stay within evidence, and the reading mode works.
- Rewrite: the named defect is repaired; required facts, qualifiers, and unaffected decisions survive.
- Handoff: the selected mode in [artifact-and-handoff.md](artifact-and-handoff.md#select-the-handoff-mode) meets its completion criterion; the next owner can locate the inputs, see preserved decisions and open issues, and understand the authorized next operation.

User approval is necessary when the next action requires it, not as a prerequisite for finishing every candidate. Report production and release evidence separately when those actions are in scope.

## Findings and repair scope

Start with the review result the user wants: findings only, proposed repairs, or revised content. For each material finding, give the page or location, observation source, observable issue, its effect on the reader, and the reading result that should improve. Use the available page number, title, region, or element to make the location findable; label a user report as reported feedback and a direct observation with the artifact or view inspected.

For findings only, describe the desired reading result and leave the concrete wording, composition, and visual solution to the recipient. For example, “On page 12, the supplied screenshot shows the three stages at equal emphasis; readers cannot identify where to start. The sequence should be apparent on first reading.” This is complete without choosing a layout or drawing a replacement.

For proposed repairs, offer concrete changes within the user's permitted scope. For revised content, use the rewrite route and preserve unaffected decisions. Carry retained user design instructions into any handoff through [artifact-and-handoff.md](artifact-and-handoff.md#select-the-handoff-mode).

A findings review is complete when each material issue is locatable, its evidence and reader impact are clear, and the recipient knows the desired improvement. Separate unverified reports from inspected findings and state any missing view needed to confirm a consequential issue. A concise review can express these in prose; use fields only when the user or consumer needs them.

## Content review

Check the actual material for:

1. Source authority and relevant versions: references were read before claiming alignment.
2. Audience task: the presentation addresses the right question and knowledge level.
3. Claim fidelity: evidence, dates, qualifiers, and intended status survive editing.
4. Narrative movement: each page advances understanding; evidence and transitions support the order.
5. Coverage: required facts are placed or their exclusion is explained; appendix and body pointers resolve.
6. Handoff usefulness: the selected mode gives the next owner usable inputs and preserves user instructions at the requested detail.

Apply cross-deck ownership, quantitative, capacity, and accessibility checks only when the deck contains those concerns. Directly review dependencies affected by changes. Use a simple page/source map for larger work; a formal graph is not compulsory.

## Language at the audience's knowledge level

Read titles, screen copy, and speaker support together for meaning. Use [prose-quality-gate.md](prose-quality-gate.md) for all applicable writing checks, selected settings, screen/speech counting boundaries and repair completion. Keep necessary visible qualifiers, source credits and distinct list items. A cleaner sentence must preserve rankings and whether events occurred together.

Screen copy states the result or explains its evidence. Cut narration about how the author assembled or arranged the slide when the audience can already see it. Explain a grouping convention only when the audience needs it to interpret the evidence; preserve the meaning in the actual categories or labels where possible.

In a reply to a reviewer, answer the requested change before repeating background they supplied; keep only the new fact needed to judge it. A standalone deck still establishes the context its audience needs. Author and production notes retain required source and review guidance outside audience copy. Page order, claim fidelity, and the agreed content owner remain authoritative.

## Presentation profiles

- Investor pitch: opportunity, mechanism, bounded evidence, business path, team facts, and a legitimate ask. Targets remain targets.
- Product presentation: user situation, task, experience, value, proof, and adoption path.
- Case study: situation, constraints, decisions, shared responsibility, observed outcome, and supported lessons.
- Strategy update: changed facts, interpretation, choices, tradeoffs, resources, and next action.
- Internal report: current state, issue, evidence, consequence, and actionable next step.
- Demo or talk: audience expectation, demonstration sequence, what each action proves, transitions, and a useful fallback.

These are emphasis choices, not compulsory chapter templates.

## Stop and continue

A material unsupported claim, unresolved source conflict, misleading omission, or unauthorized next action blocks the dependent use. Continue independent supported content and deliver a useful bounded result.

Ask for the smallest missing decision only when it changes the result and cannot be resolved from available sources. Unknown template capacity blocks a claim of native fit; it does not block drafting the story. Optional polish does not justify endless review.
